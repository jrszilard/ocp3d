OCP3D · three retrofuture direction studies · local mockups only · nothing shown is implemented or deployed
Design explorationChoose the imagined future.

These are three deliberately different visual worlds for the same evidence-first OCP3D product. Each treats the car, the part record, and the request flow as the centre of gravity—then changes the era, material, light, typography, and workshop atmosphere around them.

The prior Orbital Service / Service Assembly work is not assumed. All three remain conceptual, local-only, and easy to discard.

KEEPCar, interface, measurement, provenance, and a precise request flow remain central.
EXPLORERetrofuture can mean optimistic service infrastructure—not space, apocalypse, or a mascot.
AVOIDCopied trade dress, humanoid robots, generic neon cyberpunk, and visual atmosphere outranking evidence.
