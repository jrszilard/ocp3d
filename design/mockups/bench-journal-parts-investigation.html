TO BENCH The parts investigation / opening entry

An old-car problem leaves a trail.

Sometimes that trail begins with a broken original. Sometimes it begins with something the car never had—like a cup holder that respects the console it lives in. Either way, the claim only comes home when the physical interface is on the bench.

“The route is not the story. The evidence is.”OCP3D field rule / 01
LEG 01 / PROBLEM

Start with the car.

A broken original or an old-car annoyance—such as no useful place for a drink—can open a field file.

LEG 02 / BOUNDARY

Measure what it meets.

A reproduction starts at the original. An improvement starts at the console, trim, and what must not be blocked.

LEG 03 / ROUTE

Restore or improve.

Every file states whether it is a faithful replacement, a repair aid, or an original OCP3D accessory.

LEG 04 / PROVE

Earn the record.

Fit and function each earn their own result—never a conclusion drawn from a route, render, or story.

What expands beyond a missing part

Two part paths.
One evidence standard.

Restore: faithfully reproduce or repair what the car originally had. Improve: make an explicit OCP3D-original accessory for a real old-car annoyance—for example, a console cup holder—not a disguised factory replacement. Both begin at a measured interface and state their claims separately. Keep out: false travel claims, real rally-event or OEM branding, generic race-car imagery, performance/safety product promises, and detective-fiction theatrics.
