A future garage, built around the old car.

This is not a logo or a mascot proposal. The theme comes from a shared spatial system: cars sit in service bays; content is held in fixtures; measurement, assembly, and evidence travel on the same rails across every page.

01 / STRUCTURE

Service bays.

Rails, posts, and calibrated borders create the page skeleton. They are not illustration pasted on top.

02 / HELP

Useful assemblies.

Cradles, caddies, gantries, and layer rigs are functional workshop language — no character, face, or AI cue.

03 / TRUST

Evidence stations.

Status, dimensions, and next actions occupy fixed surfaces so the atmospheric layer never outranks the record.
