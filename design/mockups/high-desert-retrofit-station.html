A cyberpunk world with visible history. This is the lighter, drier fork of Afterlight: a roadside service workshop in a new-tech era, built on sun-faded signs, inherited lifts, old cabinets, hand-held knowledge, and equipment that has simply been upgraded again and again.
The contrast is the point: weathered mechanical infrastructure below; clean sensing, records, and additive tools laid over it—not a pristine future, not a pure nostalgia set.
THE OLD LAYERFaded enamel, analog dials, mechanical hoists, old safety glass, painted lettering, sand, heat, and repairs visible in the structure.
THE NEW LAYERCalibrated light bars, scan traces, modern material tools, solar-fed terminal surfaces, and precise evidence capture.
THE RESULTA true retrofit environment: cars and inherited equipment have been kept alive long enough to meet tomorrow.
