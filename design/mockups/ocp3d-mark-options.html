Three marks for Old Car Problems

The current fixture mark reads as a computer monitor with a line chart at header size. All three options below are drawn from the 16 px silhouette outward, work in one colour, and depict the method (measure, then make) rather than a single part or a car silhouette. That follows the rules from the earlier mark studies: no individual-part icon, no shield. A caliper version was drawn first and dropped: inside a ring, its beam, sliding head and jaws read as a firearm at large sizes.

RECOMMENDED

A · The stamp

A drafting dimension (the ←→ line on a technical drawing) over a part built in layers, inside a rubber-stamp ring. Measure, then make: one picture of the whole method. The ring ties it to the status stamps and to the "case open" roundel people already remember from the site.

It works as a favicon, a packaging stamp and a sticker. The full seal adds rim lettering for large uses.

B · Inspection initials

The letters inside a double-ring inspection stamp. Strong on its own, for example on a sticker or bag.

Next to the wordmark it repeats the name, and at 16 px the letters blur together.

C · Bench block

The same dimension and layers in a squared frame. Closest to the current mark's footprint, so it is the smallest change.
