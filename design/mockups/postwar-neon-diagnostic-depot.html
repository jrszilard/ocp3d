The stronger, darker successor to Diagnostic Centre. An original cyberpunk/postwar-retrofuture service world: rain-slick metal, phosphor instruments, degraded utility infrastructure, sodium-and-magenta light, and a car treated as a physical record—not a prop in a copied franchise world.
Reference boundary: mood-level “atomic-age future gone rough” only; no borrowed insignias, mascots, interface patterns, type, assets, or recognizable trade dress.
